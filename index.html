Xingbo Li

武汉纺织大学 · 硕士研究生（2026 级）·Wuhan, Hubei, China

我的研究兴趣在泛在计算、无线感知与智慧健康的交叉处 —— 例如用毫米波雷达与手机 IMU 做非接触式健康监测，像震颤评估、帕金森病的远程随访。

目前我在探索如何构建低负担、能在真实日常环境里跑起来的感知系统，也期待把这些兴趣落成具体的研究成果。这个页面顺便收了我平时做的一些小工具，都能直接在浏览器里用，数据不出本机。

研究兴趣

Research

I am a master's student (Class of 2026) at Wuhan Textile University. My research interests lie at the intersection of ubiquitous computing, wireless sensing, and smart health — for example, using mmWave radars and smartphone IMU sensors for non-invasive health monitoring, such as tremor assessment and remote monitoring for Parkinson's disease.

Right now I am exploring how to build practical, low-burden sensing systems that work in real daily-life settings, and I look forward to turning these interests into concrete research outcomes.
